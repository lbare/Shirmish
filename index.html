How to play

Tap a grey or rival planet to flag it. Your nearest planets send ships and keep reinforcing until it falls. Tap again to commit more (up to three dots). Tap the small × on it to cancel.

Drag from one of your planets to any target to send that planet's ships there now. Or tap your planet to select its ships, then tap a target.

Double-tap any planet to send every ship you have.

Hold one of your planets to mark it for defence. Neighbours send help whenever it is attacked.

Ships trade fire when they meet, and shoot a planet's number down to zero to take it. Planets you or a rival own shoot back; grey planets never do, but they slowly recover if left alone.

Each planet holds a limited garrison and stops building when full. Large groups at a planet are drawn as fewer, bigger ships.

Upgrade a planet you hold: select it and press Upgrade. Each of the two levels gives faster building, more health, a bigger garrison and a faster turret.

The one or two large planets on a map are tougher, build faster and hold more.

Asteroid belts (dotted rings) halve ship speed. Nebulae (soft clouds) hide the ships inside from the other side.

Planets far from the action pass their ships toward the front on their own, and stray ships drift home.
