How to play

Tap a grey or rival planet to flag it. Your nearest planets send ships and keep reinforcing until it falls. Tap again to commit more (up to three dots). Hold it to cancel.

Tap one of your planets to pick exactly its ships, then tap where they should go.

Hold one of your planets to mark it for defence. Neighbours send help whenever it is attacked.

Drag to move around the map and pinch (or scroll) to zoom. The ⤢ button switches between the whole map and a close view.

Each planet holds a limited garrison (40, 60 or 80 ships by upgrade level) and stops building when full, shown as the small count under its health.

Planets far from the action pass their ships toward the front on their own, and stray ships drift home.

Ships trade fire when they meet and shoot a planet's number down to zero to take it. Grey planets never shoot back.

Upgrade a planet you hold: select it and press Upgrade to spend ships on faster building, up to two times.
